and Your teaching is within me.

This site presents a single object.

The canon is approached here not primarily as theology, law, or narrative, but as a performative composition — a text designed to unfold in sound and time.

Across narrative, legal, and liturgical materials, the text exhibits systematic organization of rhythm, cadence, repetition, and closure. These features are not confined to explicitly marked “songs” and do not function as ornament. They operate as structural infrastructure.

Much of this organization becomes inaudible when the text is encountered only through silent reading or semantic analysis. When the text is voiced, however, patterns of pacing and resolution emerge that shape its temporal form.

This site offers no interpretation and no instruction. It does not reconstruct music and does not propose belief.

It presents the text in a condition where its form may be encountered.

Attention is the only requirement.
